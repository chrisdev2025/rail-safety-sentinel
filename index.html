What Virtellect handles

Six ways your caseload gets lighter — this quarter.

Point it at the work that eats your team’s hours. Everything runs with your rules, your review, and a clear trail back to the source.

01

Intake that never sleeps

Answers the call, text, or web lead at 2 a.m., captures the facts of loss, screens for PIP and coverage, and books the sign-up or first visit — so no case, and no patient, walks.

02

Records, chronologized

Turns an 800-page record dump into a clean treatment chronology with providers, diagnoses, and specials — same day, every citation traceable to the page it came from.

03

Demands, drafted

Assembles the first draft from the file — liability demands with a tallied specials ledger for firms, PIP demand letters with the unpaid-claim ledger for clinics — ready to sharpen and send.

04

The deadline guardian

Tracks statutes of limitations, PIP filing windows, payer response clocks, and treatment gaps — and escalates before anything gets close, so a missed date never becomes a lost claim.

05

Client & patient communication

Sends status updates, checks on treatment, and answers “what’s happening with my case?” — in your voice, on time, so the people you serve feel looked after, not forgotten.

06

Claims, EOBs & liens reconciled

Tracks every claim to its EOB, flags reductions and denials with the documentation to contest them, and keeps lien, balance, and settlement math right the first time.
